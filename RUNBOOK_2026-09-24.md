## 0ac. Package ac: the revised ensemble sets, in 1-D first

ac is ab with the uncertainty ensemble's two moved sets revised
(`UNCERTAINTY_BOUNDS_REVISION_2026-10-09.md`; the member table and its
justification are in `runtime/Uncertainty_1D/README.md`). Greg's
instruction of 9 October, on the first five-year figures: much reduce
KNmaxd, and alter alpha so the band covers the upper pH side and overlaps
the observations instead of dropping below the central line; bounds
justified from literature, no unrealistic overshoot. What changes:

- **The twelve biogeochemistry literals** move a factor of two around the
  shipped values (fd by 0.1; pepf and Vdb clipped to Table S2) instead
  of sitting at Table S2's ends. The first run's upper set, every literal
  at the table's top on pools spun up at the shipped values, mineralised
  about a decade of litter in 2016 (+865 g C/m2, 145 kg N/ha of nitrate,
  36 kg N2O-N/ha before any fertiliser, control pH 6.1 to 4.9): a spin-up
  transient, not a parameter uncertainty.
- **KNmaxd** runs 0.002-0.0064 d^-1 (was 0.002-0.01): the per-year chamber
  fits around the calibrated 0.0048.
- **Alpha** stays 1.3 / 3.4 / 6, the exchange constants slid as before.
- **Assignment by the direction of the basalt pH response**: the lower
  set is the fast-nitrogen, low-alpha, slow-weathering set (literals x2,
  KNmaxd 0.002, alpha 1.3, dissolution factor 0.8, s^2) and the upper set
  the slow-nitrogen, high-alpha, fast-weathering one (literals x0.5,
  KNmaxd 0.0064, alpha 6, 1.2, sqrt(s)). Only the literals' side flips
  against the first run. The tags `lower/` and `upper/` and every
  controller, batch file, task, driver and model file are byte for byte
  ab's; the original set is untouched.

## Known and accepted

* Defect A, revised: against August the control sits within the two
  observed layers except in 2017 and 2018, when every plot and depth rose
  0.3 together. Its five-year decline (0.22) lies between the layers'
  trends. Not worked on; `budget.m` on the n control shows whether the
  salt closure holds its acid budget near m's.
* Modelled ET is low against the towers at free drainage. Accepted as an
  ERA5 limitation.
* Validation and projections share one forcing basis: the SSP forcing
  reproduces the legacy de-accumulation, so its 2016-2020 rain and
  shortwave equal `Data_Kantola.mat` hour by hour (the build checks this).
  Over 2016-2020 they still differ in vapour pressure (the builder takes
  `ea` from the dew point; the legacy `ea` is 42 Pa lower on average and
  up to 381 Pa off), in the direct/diffuse split (the legacy split does
  not close to its own total) and in CO2 (pathway against `Ca_Data.mat`,
  at most 1.1 ppm).
* Defect B, diagnosed: in m the August basalt-minus-control effect is
  right to within 0.13 of the 0-30 cm depth-weighted observation through
  2018, then 2.4 times too strong in 2019 and 4.1 times in 2020, as the
  exchanger saturates (f_H 4 % in 2019, 0.7 % in 2020; the field stayed at
  83 to 90 % base saturation). Cause and fix in
  `LEACHING_CLOSURE_REVIEW_2026-10-01.md`; the sweep of section 4a with
  base saturation scored is the remaining half.
